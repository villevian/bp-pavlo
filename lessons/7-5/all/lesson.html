Business workshop

3A Read the situation and the three offers.

📌 The big contract

You are Pavlo, a project manager at BrightCode, an IT studio in Kyiv. GreenBox is an online grocery delivery company in Kyiv, Lviv and Odesa. They want a new mobile app, an online shop and a payment system. Their budget is about €40,000, and they need the first version before the spring sale — in 10 weeks.

Three companies sent offers. On Friday every company gives a 5-minute online presentation. Then GreenBox chooses one.

Business workshop

B Prepare. Compare the three offers and answer the questions. Use comparatives and superlatives.

Which company looks the cheapest? Is it really the cheapest? Why / why not?

Which company is the fastest? Can every company finish before the spring sale?

Which company is the biggest? Is the biggest always the best?

What are BrightCode’s three best arguments?

What is BrightCode’s weak point? What can you say about it?

1 AppNova looks the cheapest (€29,000), but there is an additional fee for the payment system (€6,000) and a monthly fee for support (€900). In the first year it costs €45,800 — more than BrightCode. · 2 BrightCode is the fastest (8 weeks). AppNova needs 10 weeks — that’s very close to the sale. WebGiant needs 16 weeks, so they can’t finish before the sale. · 3 WebGiant is the biggest provider in Europe, but it is the most expensive and the slowest. · 4 the best value for money (fixed fee, no hidden fees) · the fastest · the most satisfied customers (4.8) · 5 We aren’t the cheapest on paper and we aren’t the biggest — but our fee includes everything, and we have more experience than AppNova.
Presentation

4Give your presentation online (5 minutes). Open the slides, share your screen and present BrightCode’s offer to Olena (your teacher). Under every slide there is a task: what to say, what to compare and which words to use. There is no script — speak!

🎤 Open the presentation

Remember: greet → the aim and the plan → the company → services → price → speed and quality → why us → questions. Use First / Then / Let’s move on to / As you can see and compare: cheaper than, the fastest, the best value for money.

After the presentation: write 2–3 things your teacher said you did well, and 1–2 things to improve.

Speaking

5Question and answer session. Olena asks you questions. Answer them — use the words you didn’t use in your presentation.

  1. Why should we pay more than AppNova’s price?
  2. WebGiant is the biggest provider in Europe. Why are you better?
  3. What does your fee include exactly?
  4. Are there any hidden fees?
  5. What happens if you are late?
  6. Can we cancel the contract?
  7. Can you give us a free trial or a discount?
  8. Who will be our contact person?

Useful: That’s a good question. · Our fee includes … · There are no hidden fees. · We can … · If we are late, … · I’ll send you the details in an email.

Which question was the most difficult? Write your best answer to it.
