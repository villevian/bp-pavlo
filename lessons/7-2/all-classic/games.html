Speaking

5Fair or hidden? Decide, then say why in one sentence.

1. The website shows €12 a month. At the end, you see a €3 “service fee”.
2. A course costs €200. The fee includes all videos and a certificate.
3. You can cancel any time, with no cancellation fee.
4. The plan is “€8 a month” — but in the small print, you must stay for 12 months.
5. A cheaper plan exists, but you only see it when you try to cancel.
6. Extra storage costs €5 a month. It’s on the pricing page.
7. A concert ticket is €60. On the last page, they add a €12 booking fee.
8. You pay €80 for the year and save 15%.

Say: It’s fair because the price includes … / It’s a hidden fee because they don’t show … at the start.

Speaking

7Role-plays. Choose a card. You are A, your teacher is B. Then swap.

📞 Price increase

You use a design tool. The price went up from €10 to €15 a month. Call support.

Ask: Why did the price go up? Is there a cheaper plan? Is there a cancellation fee? Can I get a refund for this month?

Card B

Support agent: the new price includes an AI assistant. There is a ‘Basic’ plan for €9 (no AI). No refunds.

🧾 Team budget

Your team pays for 5 tools. You must cut the budget by €100 a month.

Decide together: Which tools do we really need? Which ones have hidden fees? Which plan is better value for money?

Card B

Teacher: you love one of the tools and don’t want to cancel it. Give reasons!

🤝 Setup fee

A software company wants a €500 setup fee. Ask them to remove it.

Say: We’re happy with the monthly fee, but … · Could you remove …? · If you remove the setup fee, we can sign an annual plan.

Card B

Sales manager: you can remove half of the fee if they choose the annual plan.

After the role-play: what did you agree?
