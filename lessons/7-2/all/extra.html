Saying prices

€40 an hour · €300 a month · €80 a year — we use a / an + time (= every hour / month / year).

€8 per user per month — per is more formal; it is common on pricing pages.

free of charge = free · at no extra cost = no additional fee

Say: €8 = eight euros · $2.5 billion = two point five billion dollars · £399 = three hundred and ninety-nine pounds · 10–15% = ten to fifteen percent

2. Our plan costs €8 per user .

Speaking

4Work in pairs. Role-play a support chat on a video call. Then swap roles. (With your teacher: your teacher is Student B.)

Is there a cancellation fee?What does the price include?Are there any hidden fees?Can I get a refund?I’d like to cancel my subscription.I’m afraid there is a fee of …We can offer you …
Student A — customer
You use a design tool, PixelPro (€15 a month). You want to cancel. Ask the support agent:
  • Is there a cancellation fee?
  • Is it a monthly plan or an annual plan?
  • Can I get a refund for this month?
  • What does the cheaper plan include?
Student B — support agent
Answer with this information:
  • It is an annual plan, paid monthly. Early termination fee: 50% of the remaining payments.
  • No refund for this month.
  • Cheaper option: Basic plan, €6 a month, monthly, no cancellation fee. Includes 10 projects, no AI tools.
  • Try to keep the customer: offer 2 months free.

After the role-play: Did you cancel or keep the plan? Why?
