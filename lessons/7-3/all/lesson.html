Example: My company uses Kyivstar for our phones. I’m happy with the coverage, but the business tariff is expensive.
📊 Did you know? Different reports — different “best” operators in Ukraine:
  • Ookla Speedtest (July–December 2024): Kyivstar had the best coverage and the fastest network. Speed score: Kyivstar 35.65 · Vodafone 33.38 · lifecell 29.42.
  • nPerf (2024): Vodafone had the highest total score (60,573), then Kyivstar (59,339) and lifecell (57,054). But lifecell had the fastest average download speed — 24.57 Mb/s.
So which is the best? It depends on the report!

Which operator do you use? Do you agree with the reports?

From: Iryna Koval (Office Manager)
To: Pavlo
Subject: Mobile operator survey — SkyMobile

Hi Pavlo,

Please find attached a copy of the customer survey you asked me to do. We compared SkyMobile with two other operators, Volt and LineUp, for our 25 company eSIMs. Please find a summary below.

Strengths
SkyMobile has:

  • the best network coverage — it works in the metro and in small towns.
  • the fastest download speed: 31 Mbps on average.
  • the best value for money: a business tariff for €9 per user per month with 40 GB.

Areas of improvement
SkyMobile has:

  • the lowest number of satisfied business clients (only 52%).
  • the longest wait for support: 25 minutes in the chat.
  • the worst problem solving: they fix only 4 problems out of 10 on the first day.

If you have any questions, please let me know.

Best regards,
Iryna

Communicative grammar
Comparing (2): Superlatives

We use superlatives to compare one thing with two or more things in the same group.

adjectivesuperlativeexample
short: cheap, fast, low, longthe cheapest, the fastestVolt is the cheapest operator.
-e: wide, safethe widestSkyMobile has the widest coverage.
-y: friendly, easythe friendliestLineUp has the friendliest staff.
one vowel + consonant: big, hotthe biggestKyivstar is the biggest operator in Ukraine.
long: reliable, knowledgeable, expensivethe most reliableIt’s the most reliable provider.
irregular: good / bad / farthe best / the worst / the furthestThey have the worst support.

Compare: 7.1 comparatives — Volt is cheaper than SkyMobile (2 things). 7.3 superlatives — Volt is the cheapest (3 or more).
