Extra practice

2You work in sales at an IT company. Write sentences for your sales presentation. Use superlatives.

Example: We / have / good / customer service → We have the best customer service.
1. Our service / be / reliable
2. We / have / knowledgeable / staff
3. We / provide / good / value for money
4. We / have / big / number of business clients
5. Our tariff / be / cheap
6. Our customers / be / satisfied / in the industry
7. We / have / high / levels of positive feedback
Speaking

3Answer the questions about you. Write short notes, then tell your teacher. Use superlatives.

1. What is the best app on your phone? Why?
2. Which is the most useful tool at your work?
3. What is the most expensive subscription you pay for?
4. Who is the most knowledgeable person in your team?
5. What is the worst thing about your internet at home?
6. Which is the fastest way to get to your office?
Speaking

4Provider cards. Your company needs a new provider. Your teacher has cards for two other companies. Ask questions and decide which company is the best. Use: Which one is the cheapest? Which has the best …? Is it the most reliable?
