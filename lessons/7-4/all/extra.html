Video

4In IT teams, a sprint review (or sprint demo) is a short presentation: the team shows what they did in the last two weeks. Watch the video from Atlassian (the company that makes Jira) and answer the questions.

▶ Watch: What is a sprint review? opens YouTube · you can turn on subtitles

1. Who comes to a sprint review?
2. What does the team show?
3. Is it a long, formal presentation or a short, friendly meeting?
4. Do you have meetings like this at work? Who presents?

Imagine you are presenting a sprint review. Write 3–4 sentences with phrases from the lesson.

Speaking

5Speed-presenting. Think of a company you know well (or would like to work for). Answer the questions in notes. Then click a card, finish the sentence about your company and say it to your teacher. Click the next card. Give as much information as you can in 2 minutes.

How old is your company? Where are you based?
What do you do? How big is your company?
What are your main products and services?
What else can you do? Where can you deliver?
