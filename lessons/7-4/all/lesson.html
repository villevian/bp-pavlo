Presenting

Click 🔊 to listen and repeat.

Starting

Good morning / afternoon, everyone.
I’m … I’m Sales Director at …
Thank you for attending this presentation.

Giving the aim and the plan

The aim of today’s presentation is to …
First, I’ll tell you … Then, I’ll explain … Finally, …
We’ll have a question and answer session at the end.

The main part

So, let’s start with …
We’re based in … / We started in …
We provide a range of … / We can …
Let’s move on to …
Now let’s talk about …

Presenting online

Let me share my screen.
Can you all see my screen?
As you can see on this slide, …
Sorry, I think I’m on mute.
I’ll put the link in the chat.

Ending

So, that’s the end of my presentation.
Thank you for listening.
Does anyone have any questions?
