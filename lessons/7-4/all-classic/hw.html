Reading

3A Read Iryna’s script. Answer the questions.

🎤 Script — QuickBox

Good afternoon, everyone. I’m Iryna, the Sales Manager at QuickBox. Thank you for joining this call. The aim of today’s presentation is to show you how we can deliver your orders.

So, let’s start with the company. We’re based in Lviv, and we started in 2021 with five couriers on bikes. Today we have 120 couriers and offices in Lviv and Ivano-Frankivsk. We deliver more than 3,000 orders a day.

Let’s move on to our services. We provide same-day delivery in the city: you get an order at 10, the customer gets it at 4. We can also pick up returns, and your customers can follow the courier on a map in our app.

Our prices start at 80 hryvnias per order. For shops with more than 500 orders a month, the price is 65 hryvnias.

So, that’s the end of my presentation. Thank you for listening. Does anyone have any questions?

1. Where is QuickBox based?
2. When did the company start?
3. How many couriers does it have today?
4. How many orders does it deliver a day?
5. What is the price per order for a big shop (500+ orders a month)?

Writing

5Write a short script (80–120 words) about your company or a company you know. Use the five stages and the phrases from the lesson. Next lesson you will present it online.

  • Start: Good morning, everyone. I’m … Thank you for joining this call.
  • Aim and plan: The aim of today’s presentation is to … First, … Then, … Finally, …
  • Company: So, let’s start with … We’re based in … We started in …
  • Services: Let’s move on to … We provide … We can …
  • End: So, that’s the end of my presentation. Does anyone have any questions?
